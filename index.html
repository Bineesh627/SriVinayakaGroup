You can book your seats directly through our official Bitla booking engine using the "Book Sleeper Bus" buttons on our website. You can select single or double berths, choose your preferred boarding/dropping points, and complete instant online payment.

Standard passenger luggage up to 15 kg per berth is free of charge. For commercial freight or heavier parcels, our Express Logistics desk charges economical per-kg rates starting at ₹10–₹15/kg with optional cargo insurance.
