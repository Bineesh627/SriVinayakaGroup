About Sri Vinayaka Group

Driven by Trust. Connecting Bangalore, Coorg & South India with premium mobility and express logistics.

Our Heritage & Roots

Redefining Regional Connectivity with Integrity & Excellence

Sri Vinayaka Group is an established name in South India's passenger mobility, express freight, and holiday travel sector, with registered corporate headquarters at Virajpet in the heart of Coorg.

Founded on the core philosophy "Driven by Trust", Sri Vinayaka Group has consistently raised the bar in road transport. Today, the group operates 4 dedicated BharatBenz and multi-axle luxury AC sleeper buses on fixed 21:30 & 21:50 daily schedules between Bangalore and Coorg, alongside a high-velocity overnight parcel logistics network and bespoke holiday charters.
