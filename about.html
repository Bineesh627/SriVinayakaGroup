Our History & Legacy

Building 15+ Years of Punctuality, Safety & Trust

Founded with a passion to redefine travel between Bangalore and Coorg, Sri Vinayaka Group has grown from humble beginnings into a premier mobility and parcel logistics brand serving South India.

Our company motto "Driven by Trust" underpins every aspect of our operations: from maintaininig ultra-hygienic sleeper berths to guaranteeing safe overnight parcel arrivals.

Safety First

GPS tracking & dual experienced drivers on all routes.

Punctual Schedules

Reliable overnight timings guaranteed.

Our Vision & Mission

To be the most trusted and customer-preferred transport and logistics brand in South India, setting benchmark standards in passenger luxury, cargo security, and sustainable technology.

  • Deliver seamless overnight connectivity between key economic and tourist hubs.
  • Ensure 100% safe, damage-free, and timely doorstep parcel logistics.
  • Continuously innovate customer convenience through digital booking systems like Bitla.

Starting as a single route operator connecting Bangalore and Madikeri, Sri Vinayaka Group has grown into a fleet of modern AC Sleeper coaches and specialized express logistics trucks serving thousands daily.
